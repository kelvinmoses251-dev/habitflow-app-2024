### Roadmap batch 4: the completion moment and motivation

| # | Area | What changed | Why |
|---|------|--------------|-----|
| R34 | Completion | Ticking a habit now has a designed moment: a ring draws round the box, the box fills, the check draws itself and the row settles, in about 0.8 seconds, once. It starts the instant you tap (it does not wait for the save), carries on smoothly when the list redraws, and the vibration follows the same rhythm. Counting habits get it on the step that reaches the goal. People who ask their device for less motion just see the box ticked | The most repeated action in the app was the plainest: a box changed colour. A completion should feel like something happened, without making anyone wait |
