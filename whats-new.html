What's new

Habitly changes often. This page lists what changed and why, newest first, in plain words.

A new front door

28 November 2026

  • The demo on the first page is now a small app with a Stats tab and a week of sample history, so you can see a streak and a chart before signing up.
  • You can sign in with a link sent to your email instead of a password.
  • New accounts are asked what they want more of, and get first habits suggested.
  • Installing Habitly on your phone has its own guide, with steps for iPhone.

Statistics, rebuilt

27 November 2026

  • Statistics is a dashboard with a Week, Month, Year and All switcher. Every figure follows it.
  • A full year of days as squares; tap one to see what was done and what was missed.
  • A weekly review in four steps, and your month and year told as a short story.
  • Suggestions drawn from your own numbers, a wall of personal records, and export to a spreadsheet.

A page for every habit

27 November 2026

  • Each habit opens as its own page with a month calendar, charts, a year view, notes you can search and focus sessions.
  • A missed day can be filled in until the end of the next day.
  • Every habit has its own link and its own share picture.

Reasons to come back

26 November 2026

  • A level page, milestones at 7, 30, 100 and 365 days, and achievements you can look through.
  • You choose when a streak shield is used.
  • An evening recap, an optional note of how a habit felt, and a choice of completion sounds.

Habits that fit real life

25 November 2026

  • Habits can repeat on chosen days or a number of times a week, count towards a goal such as eight glasses, and have a start and an end date.
  • Pause a habit for a holiday without losing its streak, or archive it.
  • A library of ready-made habits, and a reminder time for each habit.
  • Habits can be edited at any time.

A calmer Home, and a new look

24 November 2026

  • Completing a habit takes one tap, with Undo.
  • Today's list is on the first screen, with sections for morning, afternoon and evening, search, and a way to look at earlier days.
  • New typography, colours, icons and a dark theme drawn to match.
